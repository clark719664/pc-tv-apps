About these channels

Public streams from broadcasters and their distribution platforms. Availability varies by country, connection and device. Channels may include ads or stop broadcasting. This app cannot unlock restricted channels.

Search and favorites work offline after installation. Live video needs internet. The desktop library, recording and TV remote are available in separate apps.

Channel metadata: iptv-org. Playback: hls.js (Apache 2.0). Stream rights remain with their owners.

Install PC TV Live

iPhone: open in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.

Android: choose Install app or Add to Home screen from your browser’s menu.

Installation requires HTTPS. Live video needs internet.
