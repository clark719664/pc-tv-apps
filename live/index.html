About these channels

This is a small, curated selection of public streams, separate from the desktop app. No sign-in, PC, or home server is required.

Streams come from broadcasters and their distribution platforms. Availability varies by country, connection and device. Channels may include ads or stop broadcasting. This app cannot unlock restricted channels.

Search and favorites work offline after installation; live video always needs internet. There is no recording, desktop library, program schedule, Roku remote, or Chromecast sender here.

Channel metadata: iptv-org. Playback: hls.js (Apache 2.0). Stream rights remain with their owners.
